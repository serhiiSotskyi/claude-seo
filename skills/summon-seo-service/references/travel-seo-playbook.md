# Travel SEO Playbook

Load only the sections matching the client's business model.

## Business Model Detection

Classify the client before recommending architecture:

- Tour operator or activity provider
- Travel agency or specialist advisor
- Accommodation group or individual property
- Destination marketing organization
- Transport, cruise, rail, or airline
- Travel marketplace or aggregator
- Travel SaaS or B2B supplier

Do not apply marketplace-scale page patterns to a specialist operator, or local
hotel tactics to a destination organization.

## Commercial Architecture

Map demand to real inventory and commercial capability:

- Destination, route, property, tour, package, and theme pages
- Departure points, durations, dates, accessibility, and traveller types
- Informational guides that support a bookable commercial page
- Comparison and itinerary pages where the client can substantiate claims

Avoid doorway pages, empty destination templates, expired inventory, and pages
whose only variation is a place name. Canonicalize or remove combinations that
cannot provide unique value.

## Seasonality

- Compare at least 12 months where data permits.
- Separate booking window from travel date.
- Preserve useful seasonal URLs rather than recreating them annually.
- Update availability, pricing qualifiers, opening dates, and cancellation terms.
- Plan content and links before demand peaks, not during the peak.

## International And Local

- Validate language, currency, date, tax, and availability differences before hreflang.
- Use locale URLs only where localized content and servicing exist.
- Keep NAP, map, transport, and accessibility information accurate for physical locations.
- Distinguish destination intent from near-me/local pack intent.

## Inventory And Facets

- Crawl representative filter combinations and rendered inventory states.
- Define indexable combinations from measured demand and sufficient inventory.
- Use canonical, robots, links, and sitemap inclusion consistently.
- Handle sold-out and discontinued inventory with useful alternatives and stable status logic.
- Prevent internal search results and tracking parameters from becoming indexable landing pages.

## Trust And Conversion

Check:

- Operator identity, licences, protection schemes, contact details, and policies
- First-hand destination or product experience
- Named authors/reviewers and update dates for safety-sensitive guidance
- Review provenance and structured data eligibility
- Clear inclusions, exclusions, accessibility, cancellation, and pricing qualifiers
- Mobile booking friction and Core Web Vitals on inventory and checkout paths

## Structured Data

Use only types supported by the page's real content. Common candidates include
Organization, LocalBusiness subtypes, Hotel, LodgingBusiness, TouristAttraction,
Product, Offer, BreadcrumbList, Article, VideoObject, and Event where applicable.
Validate eligibility against current Google documentation and never invent ratings,
prices, availability, or locations.

## PPC-To-SEO For Travel

Use paid search terms to identify commercial language, then validate with GSC,
GA4, DataForSEO, inventory, and seasonality. Prioritize repeated non-brand intent
with conversions and weak organic coverage. Reject terms that rely on unavailable
inventory, misleading location claims, or paid-only promotional urgency.

## AI Visibility

Measure observed mentions and cited sources. Improve sourceable facts, entity
consistency, first-hand expertise, comparison methodology, and original data.
Treat crawler controls and `llms.txt` as access/experimentation topics, not proof
of inclusion or ranking in an answer engine.
