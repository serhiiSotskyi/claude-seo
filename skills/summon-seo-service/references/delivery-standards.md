# Summon SEO/GEO Delivery Standards

## Evidence Record

Record these fields for every material finding:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Observation | Verifiable fact, metric, or rendered behaviour |
| Source | Tool, property/site/account, URL, or client document |
| Date | Collection date and data range for volatile sources |
| Interpretation | Why the observation matters |
| Recommendation | Specific change, owner, URL, and dependency |
| Confidence | High, medium, or low with a short reason |
| Falsifier | Result that would show the recommendation was wrong |
| Measurement | Leading indicator, outcome metric, and review date |

Do not turn a hypothesis into a fact. Mark missing data explicitly.

## Confidence

- **High:** multiple independent sources agree, or one authoritative first-party
  source directly measures the issue.
- **Medium:** one reliable source plus a defensible inference.
- **Low:** incomplete access, small samples, conflicting sources, or an untested
  assumption. Low-confidence work requires validation before implementation.

## Priority

- **Critical:** blocks crawling, indexation, measurement, or a key commercial journey.
- **High:** meaningful revenue or visibility opportunity with strong evidence.
- **Medium:** useful optimization after dependencies are resolved.
- **Low:** exploratory, cosmetic, or weakly evidenced.

Priority is not impact alone. Consider impact, confidence, effort, reversibility,
dependency, and time to evidence.

## Review

Before client delivery:

1. Confirm every material number has a source and date range.
2. Reproduce critical findings manually or with an independent check.
3. Remove generic recommendations that are not tied to a URL or decision.
4. Confirm proposed schema and technical changes match current Google guidance.
5. Check travel claims, prices, availability, and legal wording against client sources.
6. Separate SEO outcomes from PPC, direct, referral, and brand demand.
7. Confirm AI visibility claims describe observed citations or mentions, not promised rankings.
8. Assign an owner and acceptance criteria to every approved action.

Use a qualified reviewer for the first five external baselines and for high-risk
migrations, penalties, international architecture, or large programmatic launches.
